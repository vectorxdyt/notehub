# Notehub design

A quiet lesson archive for reviewing board photographs after class. Preserve warm paper, forest green, and restrained yellow accents.

## Library surface
Mode: Operate. Photographs and lesson titles lead. Use Manrope for headings and DM Sans for controls. Calculus is the sole course. Desktop lesson destinations live in collapsible unit groups in the left rail and retain exact Math Medic lesson numbers, including 6.10. BC topics use distinct IDs. On phones, a grouped lesson selector replaces the full sidebar tree. Selecting a lesson filters notes and preselects the upload destination.

Three columns on wide desktop, two on smaller desktop and tablets, one generous photo card on phones. Metadata remains readable at every breakpoint. Use topic color sparingly, with a consistent tag and dot per topic. Latest-lesson markers stay in the top right, away from typical board titles.

## Interaction
Visible focus, readable contrast, generous touch controls, palette-matched selection and scrollbars. Lesson cards expose a clear View lesson action and a separate bookmark. Native dialogs preserve keyboard focus and close from the backdrop or the explicit close action, never from clicks on interior padding. Photo viewers preserve the full image and support zoom. Respect reduced motion.

## Content
Example notes are labeled. Avoid decorative headings, repeated sentimental labels, and ornamental symbols. Shared storage and teacher authentication behavior remain unchanged.
